# Code of conduct

Oriw is built by and for a community. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or events) agrees to:

- Be kind and respectful, especially when you disagree.
- Welcome newcomers and people of every background, identity and level of experience.
- Give and accept feedback on work, never on people.
- Not harass, insult, threaten or exclude anyone, and not share others' private information.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, report it privately to the maintainers through a private message to an organization owner on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

## What happens

Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, in any Oriw space.
