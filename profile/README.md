**Think big. Stay fast. Keep it yours.**

Oriw (said OR-ee-w) is a fast, private, open-source whiteboard for Windows, macOS and Linux. Boards stay encrypted on your device, teams collaborate computer to computer with no cloud required, and a built-in AI agent works with the model you choose: Claude, GitHub Copilot, Gemini, or Llama on your own computer.

- Free for individuals, teams and startups, with no limits
- Viewers, guests and clients are always free
- Works fully offline; no account needed
- No seats, no AI credits, no lock-in

**Status:** public preview 0.1.9 is in progress. The app's source code opens with the preview.
